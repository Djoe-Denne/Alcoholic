# Tag Taxonomy

Use at most five canonical tags per page. Tags are lowercase and hyphenated.

## Domains

- `minecraft` — Minecraft mod development and gameplay systems.
- `software-architecture` — Architectural boundaries and design decisions.
- `compatibility` — Cross-mod, loader, or version interoperability.
- `testing` — Automated verification and quality controls.

## Types

- `type/project` — Project overview or status page.
- `type/concept` — Explanatory concept or design page.
- `type/procedure` — Repeatable technical workflow.

## Projects

- `project/alcoholic` — Knowledge specific to the Alcoholic mod.

## Aliases

- `architecture` → `software-architecture`
- `tests` → `testing`
