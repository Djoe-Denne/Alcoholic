# Brewing Kettle Visual

`brewing_kettle` left the `cube_all` placeholder. Java Block/Item, master 512, 64 in the mod, packs downsampled from the master.

## Front instruments (−Z)

After the first copper colour was accepted, the user asked for more detail, not a new palette:

- Right pressure gauge: cream dial, red zone, needle at 45°
- Left thermometer: same dial language, needle at −22.5°
- Sight glass between them (amber liquid)
- Tube thermometer on the east flank
- Whistle / vent on the lid
- Cross wheel on the tap
- Iron and copper rivets on the back

Dial and glass tiles are painted large enough to survive 64×64.
